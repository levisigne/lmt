Hi, I'm Levi

I'm a Licensed Massage Therapist based in the Anchorage area, and I specialize in full body relaxation. I work with clients to relieve tension, improve mobility, and promote overall wellness.

My path to bodywork started at a desk. After years of feeling my own body slowly collapse under the strain of sedentary work, I decided to take my wellness seriously, and made a shift that changed how I relate to movement and self care, leading me to this career. Today, I draw on bodywork techniques from various modalities and cultures to accomplish deep theraputic results.

When I’m not in the treatment room, you can usually find me exploring movement, hanging out with my partner, or getting lost in a good story.

What I offer

Tension is a normal response to stress.

When life stress is experienced at a manageable level, the resulting tension grows strength in the body over time. Experiencing a certain amount of stress is necessary to mature.

But if the stress is too intense, it either causes immediate injury, or the body becomes weak and sick over time. Something has to give. As your massage therapist, my goal is to help you acknowledge and relieve your tension before it becomes too overwhelming.

Sometimes the tension in your body is really deep. But getting to it doesn't have to be intense. It's my belief that letting go of deep-rooted tension feels best when it's done with generous timing and grace. When you book with me, you can expect slow movement, open communication, and gentle release.

If you want to improve your sleep, mobility, pain levels, and mood, reach out to me today. Relax. You deserve it.

Good faith

The best bodywork is done in an environment that is both respectful and accepting.

Hygiene is important. I can perform bodywork both over the clothes and directly on the skin. Clients are expected to do their best to be relatively clean for appointments, and I will only accept a house call if the client can provide a relatively clean and tidy space for the massage to take place.

This is a professional service. Mutual safety and comfort is paramount. I will always do my best to be good to you, and I would appreciate the same gesture. Inappropriate and disrepectful behavior will not be tolerated.
